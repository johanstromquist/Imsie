# Shakespeare Part I: Love, Identity & Disguise - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Renaissance theater aesthetic with romantic warmth

- Art style reference: Elizabethan portrait painting meets theatrical illustration
- Inspiration: Nicholas Hilliard miniatures, John Gilbert Victorian Shakespeare illustrations, Arthur Rackham fairy tale art
- Tone: Theatrical, romantic, accessible - lighter than tragic Parts II & III
- Period: Elizabethan England (1558-1603), Renaissance theater
- Setting: Globe Theatre, Verona, Illyria, Messina, enchanted forests, Athens

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #7e1e3e (Rich burgundy - theater curtains, romance, Renaissance velvet)
- Secondary: #d4a82e (Renaissance gold - theater lighting, costume accents, warmth)
- Accents: Deep forest green, twilight blue, warm candlelight yellow
- Overall feel: Warmer and lighter than the dark tragedies of Parts II & III

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts should follow this structure to maintain consistency:

- **Tone prefix:** "Renaissance theater illustration in Elizabethan style:"
- **Content description:** [Subject, setting, mood, characters]
- **Style suffix:** "warm romantic lighting, theatrical atmosphere, detailed period costume, burgundy and gold accents, accessible Renaissance aesthetic, --ar [ratio] --style raw"

**Character Design Notes:**
- Elizabethan costumes (doublets, ruffs, farthingales, elaborate sleeves)
- Expressive faces showing theatrical emotion
- Period-accurate but not overly academic
- Distinct character silhouettes for recognition
- Romantic and comedic expressions (lighter than tragic Parts II & III)
