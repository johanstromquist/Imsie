# Heroes of Greek Myth - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Frank Miller's 300 aesthetic - high-contrast dramatic graphic novel style
- Art style reference: Frank Miller's 300, Sin City graphic novels
- Inspiration: Ancient Greek pottery art, classical sculpture, dramatic chiaroscuro
- Tone: Epic, visceral, dramatic, heroic with tragic undertones

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #1A0F2E (Deep shadow black - backgrounds, dramatic shadows)
- Secondary: #CD7F32 (Bronze/copper - armor, weapons, heroic elements)
- Accent 1: #FFD700 (Rich gold - divine elements, glory, triumph)
- Accent 2: #8B0000 (Blood red - violence, passion, sacrifice)
- Accent 3: #1E3A8A (Mediterranean blue - sky, sea, divine intervention)
- Texture: Weathered stone, aged bronze, parchment

**Artistic Style Guidelines:**
All assets should feature bold, high-contrast compositions with dramatic black shadows and stark lighting. Muscular, idealized heroic figures in dynamic action poses. Stylized rather than photorealistic - emphasizing graphic silhouettes and dramatic negative space. Heavy use of bronze/gold for heroic elements, deep blacks for shadows and drama, blood-red accents for violence and passion. Mediterranean blues for divine/magical elements. Compositions should feel like panels from a graphic novel - dramatic angles, strong diagonals, powerful focal points.

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts follow this structure:
- **Tone prefix:** "Greek mythology in Frank Miller's 300 style:"
- **Content description:** [Subject, composition, mood, specific visual elements]
- **Style suffix:** "High-contrast dramatic lighting, stark black shadows, bronze and gold accents, graphic novel aesthetic, muscular heroic figures, epic composition, visceral and dramatic"

**Example:**
Greek mythology in Frank Miller's 300 style: [Scene description]. High-contrast dramatic lighting, stark black shadows, bronze and gold accents, graphic novel aesthetic, muscular heroic figures, epic composition, visceral and dramatic --ar [ratio]
