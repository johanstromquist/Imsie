# Shakespeare Part II: Power, Ambition, Jealousy & Guilt - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Dark Renaissance/Jacobean Tragedy Aesthetic

This is the **darkest visual section** of the Shakespeare trilogy. The aesthetic should evoke:
- **Chiaroscuro lighting** (Caravaggio-style dramatic contrasts)
- **Psychological expressionism** (visuals reflecting internal states)
- **Theatrical staging** (Renaissance stage atmosphere with cinematic depth)
- **Heavy, oppressive atmosphere** (contrasting sharply with Part I's lighter tone)

**Art Style References:**
- **Lighting:** Caravaggio (dramatic shadows, spotlit subjects, religious intensity)
- **Psychological depth:** Rembrandt (inner turmoil visible in faces)
- **Darkness/madness:** Goya (psychological horror, "Black Paintings" period)
- **Theatrical staging:** Renaissance theater aesthetics (Globe Theatre, but with modern dramatic intensity)
- **Color grading:** Desaturated, heavy shadows, occasional blood red or tarnished gold accents

**Tone:**
- Oppressive, claustrophobic, psychologically intense
- Dark without being modern "horror"—this is Renaissance tragedy
- Emphasis on faces (guilt, jealousy, madness visible in expressions)
- Storm imagery, blood imagery, darkness imagery
