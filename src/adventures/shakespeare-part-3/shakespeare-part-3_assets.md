# Shakespeare Part III: Betrayal, Revenge, Justice & Reconciliation - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Transitioning from darkness to light - Renaissance meets philosophical cosmos
- **Art style reference:** Victorian Shakespeare illustrations + Renaissance paintings + Classical sculpture
- **Inspiration:**
  - Kenneth Branagh's Shakespeare films (theatrical grandeur)
  - John William Waterhouse (Pre-Raphaelite Shakespeare scenes)
  - Classical Roman sculpture and architecture
  - Romantic era Shakespeare interpretations
  - Ethereal, magical lighting for The Tempest
- **Tone:**
  - Hamlet: Dark, Gothic, philosophical - shadows and candlelight
  - Julius Caesar: Classical, monumental, political - marble and bronze
  - The Tempest: Ethereal, magical, luminous - blues and silvers
  - Epilogue: Warm, timeless, reflective - golden Renaissance tones

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #2E1A47 (Deep royal purple - tragedy, philosophy, depth)
- Secondary: #C0C0C0 (Silver/moonlight - wisdom, reflection, timelessness)
- Accent: #4A5F8F (Twilight blue - resolution, peace, understanding)
- Supporting colors:
  - Hamlet: Deep purples, blacks, candlelight golds
  - Julius Caesar: Stone grays, Roman bronze, toga whites
  - The Tempest: Ocean blues, ethereal silvers, magical teals
  - Epilogue: Warm golden browns, parchment tones, Renaissance warmth

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts follow this structure:
- **Tone prefix:** "Renaissance dramatic painting style:" or "Victorian Shakespeare illustration:"
- **Content description:** Subject, composition, mood, lighting
- **Style suffix:** "--ar [ratio] --style raw --v 6.1"
