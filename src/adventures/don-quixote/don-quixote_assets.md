# Don Quixote - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Spanish Golden Age painting aesthetic
- Art style reference: Velázquez, Goya, and Baroque Spanish art mixed with romantic interpretations of Cervantes
- Inspiration: The realist tradition of Spanish painting with warm earth tones and dramatic lighting
- Tone: Romantic yet grounded, capturing both Don Quixote's idealized vision and the harsh reality of rural Spain

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #8B4513 (Saddle brown - La Mancha earth, dry plains, rustic authenticity)
- Secondary: #DAA520 (Goldenrod - Chivalric ideals, Don Quixote's imagined glory)
- Accent 1: #B22222 (Firebrick red - Passion, Spain, Don Quixote's madness)
- Accent 2: #D2691E (Chocolate - Sancho's earthiness, practical reality)

**Artistic Style Guidelines:**
All assets should balance two visual perspectives:
1. **Reality:** Gritty, realistic Spanish countryside and characters - dusty plains, worn clothing, authentic period detail
2. **Idealism:** Occasional romantic, golden-lit moments showing Don Quixote's perception - slightly softer, more heroic

Composition emphasizes contrast between Don Quixote's tall, gaunt figure and the practical world around him. Spanish architecture, landscape, and cultural details should be historically accurate to early 1600s Spain.

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts follow this structure:
- **Tone prefix:** "Spanish Golden Age:"
- **Content description:** [Subject, composition, mood]
- **Style suffix:** In the style of Diego Velázquez and Francisco Goya, warm earth tones and dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, historically authentic with romantic elements, educational and accessible --ar [ratio]

**Example:**
Spanish Golden Age: [Scene description]. In the style of Diego Velázquez and Francisco Goya, warm earth tones and dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, historically authentic with romantic elements, educational and accessible --ar 16:9
