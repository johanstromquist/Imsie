# The Iliad - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Frank Miller's 300 aesthetic
- Art style reference: High contrast, dramatic shadows, stylized violence, graphic novel composition
- Inspiration: 300 (2006 film), Sin City graphic novels, ancient Greek black-figure pottery, epic comic book art
- Tone: Epic, brutal, mythic, dramatic, emotionally raw

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #1a0f0a (Deep bronze-black - backgrounds, shadows, dramatic contrast)
- Secondary: #c9a961 (Spartan gold/bronze - armor, highlights, divine light)
- Accent 1: #8b0000 (Blood red - violence, passion, rage)
- Accent 2: #2c1810 (Dark bronze - mid-tones, earth, weathered materials)

**Artistic Style Guidelines:**
All assets should embrace the Frank Miller 300 aesthetic: extreme high contrast between deep blacks and bronze/gold highlights, dramatic shadows cast by warriors and architecture, stylized violence with arterial spray patterns, bold silhouettes against stark backgrounds, graphic novel panel-like composition. Merge ancient Greek pottery silhouette work with modern comic book dynamism. Epic scale warfare contrasted with intimate emotional close-ups. Faces show raw, intense emotion - rage, grief, fear, determination. Muscular, heroic physiques. Minimal background detail to emphasize figures. Heavy use of negative space.

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts follow this structure:
- **Tone prefix:** "In the style of Frank Miller's 300, graphic novel aesthetic:"
- **Content description:** [Subject, composition, mood]
- **Style suffix:** "cinematic, epic scale, ancient Greek heroic art, bold silhouettes, intense emotion --ar [ratio]"
