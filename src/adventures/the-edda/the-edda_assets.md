# The Edda - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Early 20th century Scandinavian illustration inspired by Norwegian National Romanticism and Art Nouveau
- Art style reference: John Bauer's mystical forest scenes, Gerhard Munthe's decorative tapestry designs, Lorenz Frølich's Norse mythology illustrations
- Inspiration: Norwegian National Romanticism movement (1890-1920), Art Nouveau decorative elements, authentic Viking Age material culture
- Tone: Epic, mystical, dramatic yet accessible, historically evocative with fairy-tale wonder

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #1a2838 (Deep twilight blue - backgrounds, night skies, mystical atmosphere)
- Secondary: #d4a82e (Norse gold - divine elements, highlights, runic inscriptions)
- Accent 1: #8b4c39 (Runic brown - earth, wood, ancient elements)
- Accent 2: #4a6741 (Deep forest green - Yggdrasil, natural world)
- Accent 3: #c5d8e8 (Pale ice blue - frost, ethereal elements)

**Artistic Style Guidelines:**
All assets should blend the romantic, mystical quality of John Bauer's troll paintings with the decorative, stylized approach of Gerhard Munthe's Norse tapestries and the dramatic narrative clarity of Lorenz Frølich's mythological illustrations. Compositions should feature:
- Dramatic lighting with strong contrast
- Decorative Norse knotwork borders or integrated patterns
- Stylized natural elements (particularly trees, mountains, and water)
- Epic scale with attention to atmospheric perspective
- Period-appropriate depictions of Viking Age clothing, architecture, and artifacts
- Rich, saturated colors with golden accents
- Mix of realistic and fantastical elements

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts follow this structure:
- **Tone prefix:** "Norse mythology artwork:"
- **Content description:** [Subject, composition, mood]
- **Style suffix:** "In the style of John Bauer, Gerhard Munthe, and Lorenz Frølich, Norwegian National Romanticism with Art Nouveau decorative elements, dramatic atmospheric lighting, rich colors with golden accents, educational and epic"

**Example:**
Norse mythology artwork: [Scene description]. In the style of John Bauer, Gerhard Munthe, and Lorenz Frølich, Norwegian National Romanticism with Art Nouveau decorative elements, dramatic atmospheric lighting, rich colors with golden accents, educational and epic --ar [ratio]
