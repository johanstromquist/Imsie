# The Odyssey - Asset Requirements

## Overview

**Visual Style:** Frank Miller's 300 aesthetic - dramatic, high-contrast imagery
- Art style reference: Bold inking, dramatic shadows, limited color palette
- Inspiration: Frank Miller's 300, ancient Greek pottery art, heroic murals
- Tone: Epic, dramatic, heroic, visceral
- Color treatment: Burnished bronze/sepia tones with deep blacks, occasional blood red accents

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #1a0f0a (Deep bronze-black)
- Secondary: #c4793b (Burnished bronze/copper)
- Accents: #8b0000 (Dark crimson), #d4af37 (Gold), #2a1810 (Deep brown)

**Prompt Format:**
All image generation prompts follow this structure:
- **Tone prefix:** "Frank Miller 300 style illustration, black and bronze:"
- **Content description:** [Subject, composition, mood]
- **Style suffix:** "bold inking, dramatic shadows, burnished bronze and sepia tones, heroic composition --ar [aspect-ratio] "
